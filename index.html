Charge flow and band alignment in a lead-free perovskite/ACIGS 2T tandem cell

Every absorber generates electron-hole pairs. The staggered band offsets decide where each carrier can go.

What the band diagram shows

  • Cs₂TiBr₆ has a valence band 0.5 eV below MASnI₃. Holes cannot enter it, so only electrons reach FTO. It behaves like an electron-transport layer.
  • Cs₃Bi₂I₉ has a conduction band 0.8 eV above MASnI₃. Electrons cannot enter it, so only holes reach the recombination layer. It behaves like a hole-transport layer.
  • Because the outer absorbers do both jobs, the top cell needs no separate ETL or HTL.
  • In the bottom cell, the deep valence band of CdS and ZnO blocks ACIGS holes from the front side, so they go to Au. Electrons climb a small CdS spike and reach the recombination layer.
  • Top-cell holes and bottom-cell electrons recombine in the middle layer. This joins the sub-cells in series, so the voltages add.
